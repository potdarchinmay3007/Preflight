# Ideal Customer Profile Definition (mock document)

## Purpose
The ideal customer profile, or ICP, tells agents which companies are worth researching and contacting. Agents should use it to rank accounts and to decide when a buying signal is relevant.

## Firmographics
The primary ICP is a B2B software company with between 20 and 500 employees, headquartered in North America or Western Europe, that has raised a seed round or later. Companies that are agencies, consultancies or government bodies are out of scope.

## Buyer personas
The main buyers are the Head of Growth, the Head of Revenue Operations and the VP of Sales. Secondary contacts are GTM engineers and sales operations managers. Agents should prefer a named persona over a generic company inbox.

## Trigger signals
Strong signals include a job posting for a GTM engineer or revenue operations role, a recent funding round, a new sales leader joining in the last 90 days, and a public launch of a new product line. Weak signals include website visits and newsletter opens, which show curiosity but rarely show buying intent. Signals should be backtested on past data before agents act on them at scale.

## Disqualifiers
Do not pursue companies with fewer than 10 employees, companies with no website, or companies that were disqualified in the last 180 days.

## Scoring
Accounts receive up to 100 points: 40 for firmographic fit, 30 for persona match and 30 for fresh signals. Agents should only start outreach above 60 points.
