# Messaging Guidelines for Outbound Agents (mock document)

## Purpose
These guidelines keep automated messages short, honest and useful. They apply to every email or message drafted by an agent.

## Length and structure
Emails must be under 120 words. Use a subject line of 50 characters or fewer, one clear idea, and one question or call to action at the end. Avoid attachments and more than one link.

## Personalisation
The first sentence must reference something real about the account, such as a recent hire, a product launch or a public post. Never invent facts. If the research step found no reliable detail, the agent must not send a personalised message and should flag the account for human review instead.

## Tone
Write like a helpful colleague. Avoid hype words, exclamation marks, fake urgency and phrases such as limited time. Do not claim a relationship that does not exist, and never pretend to be a human rep.

## Claims
Only use customer names, numbers and quotes that appear in the approved proof points list. Never promise results. Do not mention competitors by name.

## Opt out
Every outbound email must include a plain sentence that lets the reader opt out. When someone opts out, the agent must record it within one hour so that the suppression policy takes effect.

## Review
New message templates need approval from a human reviewer before the first live run. Agents may adjust wording within an approved template, but they may not change the offer.
