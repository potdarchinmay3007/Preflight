# Contact Frequency Policy (mock document)

## Purpose
Prospects who receive too many messages stop replying and often complain. This policy sets the minimum gap between automated touches so that outreach stays useful and welcome.

## The seven day rule
No account may be contacted by an automated agent if it was contacted by anyone, human or agent, in the last 7 days. The clock starts from the last logged touch in the CRM, including emails, calls, meetings and LinkedIn messages. If the last touch is 7 or more days old, the account is eligible again.

## Why seven days
Sales reps follow up on warm conversations within a week. An automated message that arrives in the middle of a rep conversation looks careless and has led to lost deals. Seven days gives reps room to work without agents interrupting.

## Exceptions
Inbound replies are not outreach. When a prospect writes first, an agent may answer within the same day, and that answer does not count against the seven day rule. Event follow-ups sent within 48 hours of an event are also exempt, but they must be approved by a human before sending.

## Sequences
A multi-step sequence counts as one touch per step. Steps must be spaced at least 7 days apart. An agent that wants to speed up a sequence must ask a human owner first.

## Launch rule
A run that includes accounts contacted in the last 7 days is blocked. Remove those accounts or wait until the gap has passed, then simulate again. Never override this rule by editing the last contacted date.
